🇮🇳 TODAY'S TOP 10

Wednesday, 4 November 2026 | 10 News. 10 Minutes. Better Informed.

India First. People First.
The 10 developments that matter to ordinary Indians today.

1. Cyclone Murasu weakens, but heavy rain risks remain

Category: Weather & Public Safety

Cyclone Murasu has weakened after affecting coastal Andhra Pradesh. Heavy rainfall and local flooding risks remain important concerns for parts of southern and eastern India. Residents should follow local administration and IMD advisories.

Source: India Meteorological Department

2. Delhi-NCR air pollution reaches dangerous levels

Category: Health & Environment

Delhi's air quality remains in the Very Poor category, with some monitoring stations recording Severe levels. GRAP-II restrictions are in force. Children, elderly people and those with respiratory conditions should take extra precautions.

Source: IITM Air Quality Early Warning System

3. Voters missing from draft electoral rolls can submit Form 6

Category: Citizens' Rights

The Election Commission has clarified that previously enrolled voters whose names are missing from draft electoral rolls can submit Form 6 for inclusion. Check your name and follow the official election office process.

Source: Akashvani News

4. Centre proposes stricter checks for medicine manufacturing

Category: Healthcare

The Health Ministry has proposed requiring qualified personnel to authorise the release of medicine batches. The proposal aims to improve accountability and quality control. It is a proposal, not yet a confirmed implemented rule.

Source: Akashvani News

5. Pune Metro projects worth ₹21,800 crore announced

Category: Transport & Infrastructure

Major Pune Metro projects have been inaugurated or had foundation stones laid. The expansion is intended to improve urban connectivity and public transport. Actual commuter benefits will depend on project completion and operations.

Source: Akashvani News

6. Scholarship application deadline extended to 15 November

Category: Education

The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme application deadline for 2026–27 has been extended to 15 November. Eligible students may receive ₹12,000 per year. Applicants should complete registration and ensure their applications are verified.

Source: Akashvani News

7. India signs ₹53,880 crore contracts for eight Navy corvettes

Category: Defence & National Security

The Defence Ministry has signed contracts for eight next-generation corvettes for the Indian Navy. The programme supports maritime security and domestic shipbuilding. Its benefits will emerge over the construction and induction period.

Source: Akashvani News

8. Air Force schools adopt a Child Digital Safety Programme

Category: Children & Technology

Air Force schools have adopted a digital safety programme aimed at helping children use technology more safely. Online privacy, screen habits and protection from digital risks are increasingly important for families.

Source: Akashvani News

9. More than 21 lakh households report zero electricity bills under PM Surya Ghar

Category: Household Expenses & Energy

The government has reported that over 21 lakh households with rooftop solar installations under PM Surya Ghar have received zero electricity bills. Actual savings depend on consumption, system capacity and applicable billing arrangements.

Source: Akashvani News

10. Oil prices and global market movements remain in focus

Category: Economy & Cost of Living

International oil prices and financial market movements remain important to India's economy. Changes in crude oil prices can influence transport, fuel costs and inflation, although domestic fuel prices do not automatically change with every global movement.

Source: Akashvani News
